Become a Partner

We collaborate with schools, colleges, healthcare institutions, and fellow NGOs to co-host mental health literacy programs, teacher training workshops, and youth support circles.

Partnership Expectations & Requirements

• Commitment to student mental health and emotional well-being.

• Provision of physical or digital space for collaborative workshops.

• Active participation in co-branded mental health awareness campaigns.

Become a Sponsor

Sponsoring Dimagi Kura directly enables our ground-level outreach, resource production, and school programs across remote and urban communities in Nepal.

Sponsorship Categories

Financial Sponsorship: Direct grants supporting our operational expansion and workshop logistics.

In-Kind Sponsorship: Provision of venue facilities, printing materials, or technological equipment.

Media Sponsorship: Amplifying our advocacy campaigns across broadcast and digital channels.
