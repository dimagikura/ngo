WHO WE ARE

Dimagi Kura is a youth-led nonprofit dedicated to making mental health conversations more open, accessible, and stigma-free.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Mental health should be talked about before it becomes a crisis. Everyone deserves the knowledge, support, and space to take care of their mental well-being.

WHAT WE DO

Through awareness campaigns, educational content, community activities, and meaningful conversations, we help young people learn, connect, and speak openly.

What We Do

Mental Health Awareness

Breaking the silence and spreading vital knowledge about mental well-being.

Student Education

Bringing mental health resources and workshops directly to schools.

Community Outreach

Engaging local communities to build supportive, stigma-free environments.

Awareness Campaigns

Organizing impactful digital and on-ground campaigns to drive change.

Conversations & Events

Hosting safe spaces for dialogue, sharing experiences, and active listening.

Educational Resources

Providing accessible materials and guides to help individuals cope and thrive.
